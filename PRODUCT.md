# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Municipal officials and staff use the role-based application to carry out planning, budgeting, procurement, award, and related oversight work. Suppliers and observers also have accounts for their parts of the process. Officials, including older users, need clear instructions they can return to while using the system.

## Product Purpose

ProcureNance supports the municipal workflow from development planning through budget authorization, procurement, contract award, and later delivery and payment. It keeps the required approvals and handoffs in their proper order.

## Operating Context

Staff work through distinct stages and permissions. A development plan informs annual priorities and investment programming; budget authority and procurement planning precede requisitions, solicitation, bid evaluation, and award. Users may need to look up a process while they work rather than complete formal training first.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing business actions remain controlled by each account's role and permissions.
- The Help & Guide library is available to every signed-in role, including processes outside that role's action permissions. Viewing a guide does not grant permission to perform its process.
- Guides are optional references. Watching one does not gate access to another, and any available guide can be opened directly.
- Guides use short demonstrations of the actual application interface, with clear narration, captions, visible cursor actions, and focused highlights. A clip can be longer when the process needs it.
- A guide demonstration must clearly identify illustrative data and must not create or alter live municipal records during recording.
